FAQ

Common Questions When
Switching from AutoCAD

Is ZWCAD a genuine AutoCAD alternative — or just a cheap copy?⌄
ZWCAD is a fully independent, professional-grade CAD platform developed by ZWSOFT over 25+ years. It is DWG-native and supports the same file formats, APIs, and workflow conventions as AutoCAD — but runs on its own independent engine. It is not a reskin or fork of AutoCAD. It has 1.4 million users globally and is used by 149 Fortune 500 companies.
Will my AutoCAD DWG files, templates, and LISP routines work in ZWCAD?⌄
Yes. ZWCAD has native DWG compatibility and supports the same LISP, VBA, and .NET APIs. Most routines migrate with little or no modification. Cygnas Solutions tests your existing routines as part of the onboarding process and advises on any adjustments needed.
Can I get a perpetual licence — one I own outright?⌄
Yes. ZWCAD offers perpetual licences, which means you pay once and own the software permanently. Flexible upgrade support is optional, so you choose when and how to access future versions instead of being locked into subscription-only renewal.
How long does the migration process take?⌄
Most UK firms we've worked with are operational in ZWCAD within one to two weeks — including template migration, training, and testing of existing routines. For larger teams, we stage the rollout to maintain project continuity throughout the transition.
What support do I get if I buy through Cygnas Solutions?⌄
UK-based technical support by phone and email, a dedicated onboarding call, migration assistance for templates and routines, workflow-specific training, and ongoing licence management support. We remain available after purchase — we don't disappear once the invoice is paid.

Licence Models Explained

Control Your Software Investment

🏛
Perpetual Licence
Pay once and own the software permanently. No annual subscription required. Upgrade timing is under your control, so your CAD investment is not tied to automatic subscription renewal.
🔄
Flexible Upgrades
Add flexible upgrade support to a perpetual licence when you want access to future version updates and priority support. It keeps the ownership model intact while giving your team a smoother upgrade path.
💻
Standalone Licence
Tied to one specific machine. Suitable for individual workstations or dedicated CAD terminals where a single user works from a fixed location. Most straightforward to manage for smaller teams.
🌐
Network Licence
A pool of licences shared across your TCP/IP network, managed by a Network Licence Manager on your server. Particularly efficient for teams where not all users need simultaneous access — a pool of 8 can often serve a team of 12.
